# Voice Recording Corpus — Qwen3-TTS Fine-tuning
## 150 Phonetically Balanced Sentences — Italian

---

### Instructions for Recording

- Read each sentence aloud in a natural, steady tone — as if presenting on radio or television.
- Respect punctuation: mark commas and periods with slight pauses.
- Record each sentence as a separate audio file. Name files: `phrase_it_001.wav`, `phrase_it_002.wav`, etc.
- Wait 2 seconds of silence before and after each sentence.
- Re-record any sentence where you stumbled, coughed, or the audio was defective.
- Target pace: clear and measured — neither too fast nor too slow.
